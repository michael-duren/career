# Leetgrinder source review: days 57–84

Reviewed against `internal/leetgrinder/lessons.templ` and `internal/leetgrinder/sources_57_84.json` on 2026-09-27. The worked examples are original illustrations; the readings support the concepts and claims, not the examples' provenance.

The manifest has one introductory reading of 5–10 minutes per day, an optional paper or technical report per day, and no day exceeds 30 minutes. Existing first-reading `Optional` values are preserved. Introductory URLs are distinct in this range, and days 78–84 tell learners to read after attempting the problems. Live-document inspection found the failures listed below; an HTTP 200 alone was not accepted when it led to a paywall, redirect page, generic journal page, or unrelated PDF.
