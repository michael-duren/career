# LeetGrinder source review: days 29–56

This is the assigned GPT-5.6 Sol review of each lesson in `lessons.templ`, its introductory reading, and its deeper paper or technical report. The introductory URLs are unique within this set, take at most 10 minutes, and retain the existing first-reading `optional` value so spaced-review scheduling does not change. The scoped reading time for every day is 21 minutes or less. Every row below records the Sol verdict for that lesson.
